    Questions

    Do I need to be home?

    No. Most customers leave a key in a lockbox. We'll text you when we arrive and when we leave.

    What if I'm not happy with the clean?

    Tell us within 24 hours and we'll come back to fix it for free.

    Can I change or cancel my booking?

    Yes, free of charge up to 24 hours before. Just reply to your confirmation email.

    Which suburbs do you cover?

    Brisbane's northside, from Clayfield to Strathpine. Ask us if you're just outside.
