Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Who I am

This website belongs to JB, a Website Creator & Virtual Assistant based in Bogo City, Cebu, Philippines. Contact: bariljeselle@gmail.com.

What I collect

  • What you type into my booking, inquiry, project brief and payment forms: name, email, business details, links, answers, and any files you upload.
  • The call time you pick and your time zone.
  • Reviews you leave on my reviews page: your star ratings, comments, name and, if you add one, a photo or logo (resized and with location data removed). Your email and your private "what to improve" note are never shown publicly, and your review only appears publicly if you allowed it. I may remove spam or abusive reviews.
  • The services you pick on my rates (with "Add to my request"), your notes on them, and whether you confirmed them. These are sent to me only when you submit the project brief.
  • Whether you allow me to show your finished project on my Works page (asked in the project brief). You can change your answer anytime by emailing me.
  • Basic technical details your browser sends (IP address, browser type, the page you came from), used only to stop spam.

How I use it

  • To reply to you, confirm and schedule calls, send reminders, prepare quotes and deliver my services.
  • To create a calendar event and a video call link (Google Meet or Jitsi) for our calls.
  • To keep a private client record so I can follow up properly.
  • To record and transcribe our calls with tl;dv, only if you agree at the start of the call, so I don't miss any details of your project. You can say no, and I'll take notes by hand instead.

I never sell your information or use it for advertising.

Saved in your own browser

To make the site easier to use, a few things are saved only on your own device (in your browser's local storage), not sent to me:

  • The services you picked, until you submit the project brief or remove them.
  • Whether you chose to see prices in pesos or US dollars. To pick the right currency the first time, the site checks your device's time zone (for example, Philippine time shows pesos). It doesn't use your location or IP address for this.

You can clear these anytime by clearing your browser's site data. Nothing here is used for tracking or advertising.

Google account data

This website connects only to my own Google account, to add calls to my Google Calendar and create Google Meet links. It uses the Google Calendar and Google Meet permissions only for that purpose. It doesn't access, store or share any data from your Google account. The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Where it's stored

Form answers, client records and my own bookkeeping receipts are stored securely with Cloudflare, my website host. Emails are sent with Brevo. Calendar events and Meet links are in Google. Call recordings and transcripts (only with your permission) are kept in tl;dv for up to 3 months, then deleted. Each provider has its own privacy policy.

Website visits

I use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count visits and see which pages are popular. It uses no cookies, does not track you across other websites, and does not collect your name or contact details.

How long I keep it

I keep records only as long as needed to work with you, and for my business records afterwards. Email me any time to see, correct or delete your information.

Your consent

Every form on this website (booking, inquiry, project brief, payment, reviews and Works page permission) has a consent checkbox. Your details are only sent after you tick it. By ticking it, you agree that I may use the information you share for the purposes on this page. I save the date you gave consent with your message. If you share details about other people (for example, your staff or clients), please make sure they're okay with it.

Your rights

I follow the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) of the Philippines. You have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your information is used
  • Access your information and get a copy of it
  • Object to its use and withdraw your consent at any time
  • Correct information that is wrong or incomplete
  • Ask me to delete or block your information
  • Get your information in a format you can take elsewhere (data portability)
  • Claim damages and file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission (privacy.gov.ph)

To use any of these rights, email bariljeselle@gmail.com. I reply within 15 working days. Withdrawing consent doesn't undo what was already done, and I may keep records the law requires (such as payment records for taxes).
