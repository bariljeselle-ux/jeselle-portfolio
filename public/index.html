Brew it double strength

Ice melts. Stronger coffee keeps the flavor bold to the last sip.

Use coffee ice cubes

Freeze leftover coffee so your drink never gets watery.

03

Add a tiny pinch of salt

It softens bitterness and brings out natural sweetness.

04

Sweeten while it's warm

Syrup and condensed milk mix in better before the ice goes in.

05

Shake, don't stir

A quick shake makes it frothy, just like at the café.

FAQ

Questions clients often ask

What does a social media virtual assistant do?

A social media VA plans and posts your content, designs graphics in Canva, edits Reels and TikToks, writes captions and hashtags, replies to comments and messages, and sends you a monthly report, so your pages stay active without taking your time.

Why hire a virtual assistant from the Philippines?

Filipino VAs are known for strong English, a friendly and professional work ethic, and affordable rates. I communicate clearly, reply within 24 hours, and bring 7+ years of client-facing experience.

How much does a website cost?

A landing page starts at $200–$350 and a full business website at $500–$800. Website + SEO setup is $700–$1,100. Every quote is based on your scope, and I'm open to reasonable negotiation.

Do you offer monthly social media management?

Yes. Social media management is $500–$800 per month: a content calendar, Canva graphics, Reels edited in CapCut, captions, scheduling, replies and a monthly report.

Do you offer graphic design?

Yes. I design lead magnets (free guides, checklists, e-books), ad campaign creatives, social media templates, flyers and brand kits in Canva. One-time designs start at $60, and Graphic Design on Demand is $250–$450 per month.

Can you help my business show up on Google?

Yes. I set up SEO with keyword research, page titles and descriptions, image alt text and Google indexing, then keep improving it with monthly SEO management ($150–$350 per month).

Do you work with clients outside the Philippines?

Yes. I'm based in Cebu, Philippines and work remotely with clients worldwide. When you book a call, the calendar shows times in your own time zone.

How do we start?

Book a free 15-minute call and you'll get a Google Meet link right away. You can also fill in the project brief, and I'll reply within 24 hours with a plan and quote.

How can I pay?

GCash or Maya (QR code) for clients in the Philippines, and Remitly for clients abroad. I send a private payment link after you accept the quote.
