FAQ

Questions clients often ask

Do you manage social media accounts?

No. I design your social media graphics, templates, ads and Reels in Canva and CapCut, ready for you or your team to post. Posting, scheduling and replies are not included.

Why hire a virtual assistant from the Philippines?

Filipino VAs are known for strong English, a friendly and professional work ethic, and affordable rates. I communicate clearly, reply within 1 working day, and bring 7+ years of client-facing experience.

Have you worked with clients before?

I'm new to freelancing, and I'm honest about that. I bring 7+ years of client-facing experience from my previous work, and my own website runs the same booking, payment and CRM system I build for clients, so you can test it before you decide. My first 3 website clients get 10% off in exchange for an honest review.

How much does a website cost?

A landing page (1 page) is $250–$400. A business website is $850 for up to 3 pages, $1,100 for up to 5 pages and $1,400 for up to 7 pages. Every website includes online booking, a payment page, a reviews page and your own private CRM, so you don't pay monthly for separate apps. I'm open to reasonable negotiation.

How does payment work?

For one-time projects, you pay a 50% deposit to start and the other 50% before final files or launch. I accept GCash, Maya, Wise and Remitly. Full details are in my Terms of Service.

Do you offer graphic design?

Yes. I design lead magnets (free guides, checklists, e-books), ad campaign creatives, social media templates, flyers and brand kits in Canva. Designs start at $10 each, and a 10-template pack is $60–$150.

Can you help my business show up on Google?

Yes. I set up SEO with keyword research, page titles and descriptions, image alt text and Google indexing, and it's included in the Business and Premium website packages. For a website you already have, I offer an SEO audit and fix checklist ($60–$120).

Do you work with clients outside the Philippines?

Yes. I'm based in Cebu, Philippines and work remotely with clients worldwide. When you book a call, the calendar shows times in your own time zone.

How do we start?

Book a free 15-minute call and you'll get a Google Meet link right away. You can also fill in the project brief, and I'll reply within 1 working day (Mon–Fri) with a plan and quote.

How can I pay?

GCash or Maya (QR code) for clients in the Philippines, and Wise or Remitly for clients abroad. I send a private payment link after you accept the quote.
