Option 1: I host it for you

Your website runs on my hosting account. I take care of everything technical, and you pay a small monthly fee for maintenance.

Best forSmall businesses that want zero tech work
CostOne-time project fee + $50–$150/month
Handover timeSame day

What I do

  • Build your website and connect your domain
  • Host it on my account (Netlify) with free security (https)
  • Keep forms, bookings and emails working
  • Back up your site and fix problems
  • Make small updates each month (text, photos, prices)
  • Move it to your own account any time you ask

What you do

  • Buy your domain in your name (I'll guide you, about ₱600/year)
  • Send your content: logo, photos, text
  • Approve the preview
  • Pay the monthly maintenance fee
🔑 Logins you'll share: Only your domain account, once, to connect your website. We can do this together on a short call so you never need to send me your password.

Pros

  • Easiest option: nothing to set up or learn
  • Fast changes whenever you need them
  • Someone always looks after your site

Cons

  • You depend on me for changes
  • The monthly fee continues while I host it

Cons

Websites with bookings or a client dashboard

If your website includes online bookings, automatic emails or a private admin page, it also needs a free email-sending account (Brevo) and your Google account for the calendar. In options 2 and 3, I set these up in your name on a short call, so emails come from your business.

Keeping your accounts safe

  • Never send passwords by chat or email. We share them on a call.
  • Turn on 2-step verification for Google, Netlify, GitHub and your domain account.
  • Change any password you shared once the project is done.
  • I will never ask for your bank, card or GCash/Maya PIN.
