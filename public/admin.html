💰 Income records

Every payment you receive, in pesos. Use this for your own books and your BIR filing.

📝 What you must do here

Every time you get paid

  1. Record it the same day. If the client used your payment page, and tap 💰 Record as income. Otherwise tap .
  2. In , type what actually landed in your GCash, Maya or bank, after fees and conversion.
  3. Add the and attach a screenshot of the receipt with in the same form. It's saved privately with the payment. A backup copy in Google Drive ("Receipts [year]" folder) is optional.

Every month (your reminder on the 1st)

  1. Compare this list with your GCash, Maya, Wise and Remitly history (in the apps: Transactions or History). Every client payment should be here.
  2. Check the bar. Once your yearly total passes ₱250,000, income tax applies (8% of the amount above it, if you choose the 8% option).

Once you're registered with the BIR

  1. Give clients an official invoice or receipt for each payment, as the BIR requires.
  2. Every quarter and every year, tap and use the totals for your tax returns, or send the file to your accountant.
  3. Ask your BIR office (RDO 080, Mandaue City, which covers Bogo) or an accountant which forms and deadlines apply to you, and add them to your calendar.

🧾 Business expenses

What you spend to run your business, in pesos. Together with your income, this shows your real profit.

📝 What you must do here

What counts as a business expense

  1. Things you pay for because of your work: Canva Pro, your domain, internet or mobile load for work, a course, transfer fees, ads for your business, a new laptop or headset.
  2. Personal spending (food, clothes, family) does not go here.
  3. Bills you share with home (electricity, internet, rent): record only the work part. Type the and your , and the amount fills in by itself. Example: you work about 6 hours a day at home, so use about 25–30% of the electric bill. Keep the same % every month and write how you got it in Notes.
  4. Devices (laptop, phone, headset, ring light): record them on the day you buy them. If you also use them personally, use a work share % too.

Every time you pay for something for work

  1. Tap , pick the , and type the amount in pesos.
  2. Attach the receipt (photo, screenshot or PDF) with in the same form. Receipt kept ticks itself. A backup in Google Drive is optional.

Why it matters

  1. It tells you if your prices are right.
  2. If you choose the 8% option, expenses don't lower your tax, but the BIR can still ask for your records. If you choose the graduated rates, receipted expenses can lower your tax. Ask your accountant which is better for you.
