Getting your own domain

Step-by-step guide with pictures · About 20 minutes · Last updated: November 22, 2026

Your domain (like yourbusiness.com) is your website's address. You buy it in your own name, so it always belongs to you. Then you give me access to connect it, without sharing your password. The pictures below are simple drawings to show you where to tap; the real pages may look slightly different.

What you need
  • Your business email and your phone
  • A debit or credit card (about ₱700 a year for a .com)
  • One valid government ID, in case Porkbun asks to verify you
  • An authenticator app on your phone (step 7, free)

1Pick your domain name

  • Use your business name, short and easy to spell: bloomandbrew.com, not the-best-bloom-and-brew-cafe-2026.com.
  • Choose .com if it's available. It's the one customers trust and remember. .ph is great for local businesses but costs more (about ₱1,500–₱2,500 a year).
  • Avoid numbers and dashes. Say it out loud: if someone can type it after hearing it once, it's good.

2Search for it on Porkbun

  1. Open porkbun.com.
  2. Type your domain in the search box and tap the search button.
  3. Find the line with your exact domain (for example yourbusiness.com) and tap the pink +.

3Check your cart

  1. Tap the cart at the top right.
  2. Keep it at 1 year.
  3. Make sure WHOIS Privacy is on. It's free and hides your home address from the public.
  4. Don't add extras: no hosting, email, website builder or SSL. Your website and its security lock are already included with my setup.
  5. Skip "bundle" offers for .net or other endings.

4Create your Porkbun account

  1. Tap Create Your Account.
  2. Use your business email and a strong password that only you know.
  3. Type your real name and address exactly as they appear on your ID.
🪪 Porkbun may ask some new accounts to verify with a photo of a government ID (passport, driver's license, UMID or PhilSys ID) and a selfie. Use the actual card, not a photo of a screen, and make sure your account name matches your ID. Porkbun's tips

5Pay, then turn on auto-renew

  1. Pay with your card.
  2. Go to ACCOUNT → Domain Management, scroll down to your domain, and open Details.
  3. Turn AUTO RENEW to ON (green), so your domain never expires. Leave Domain Lock on.

6Verify your email and phone

  1. Open the email from Porkbun and tap the verification link. Do this within a few days, or your domain may be paused.
  2. In ACCOUNT → Account Settings, verify your phone number with the code Porkbun texts you.
🛡️ Watch out for fake "Porkbun" emails. Only click links in emails you were expecting, and when in doubt, open porkbun.com yourself and log in there.

7Turn on 2-step login (2FA)

This adds a 6-digit code from your phone each time you log in, so nobody can take your domain even if they guess your password. It's also required before you can give me access.

First, install one free authenticator app:

Google AuthenticatorAndroid (Google Play)Google AuthenticatoriPhone (App Store)Microsoft AuthenticatorAndroid and iPhone
  1. On Porkbun, go to ACCOUNT → Account Settings and find two-factor authentication. Turn on app-based 2FA.
  2. A QR code appears. In your authenticator app, tap + → Scan a QR code and point your phone at it.
  3. Your app now shows a 6-digit code for Porkbun. Type it into Verification Code on Porkbun and tap Submit.
  4. Save any backup codes Porkbun shows you somewhere safe (a notebook at home). You need them if you lose your phone.

  3. Your old link keeps working and sends visitors to the new one.
