YOUR WEBSITE + BOOKING + CRM — SETUP GUIDE
==========================================

WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
  public/            Your website pages
    index.html       Main site (portfolio, services, rates, booking calendar, inquiry form)
    brief.html       Project brief (25 questions + file uploads)
    admin.html       YOUR CRM dashboard (private, password protected)
    pay.html         Private payment page (GCash, Maya, Remitly + proof-of-payment upload)
  netlify/           The backend that sends emails, reminders and saves the CRM
  package.json       Tells Netlify which tools the backend needs
  netlify.toml       Netlify settings

HOW IT WORKS (CRM WORKFLOW)
  Client books a call
    -> Client sees a "Thank you" pop-up
    -> Client gets a CONFIRMATION email: schedule in their time zone + Philippine time,
       "Add to Google Calendar" button and a calendar invite file (Apple/Outlook)
    -> You get a "New booking" email with every detail + calendar invite
    -> Saved in your CRM (admin.html) with status "Call booked"
    -> That time slot disappears from the calendar (no double booking)
    -> 24 hours before: reminder email to the client AND to you
    -> 1 hour before:   reminder email to the client AND to you
  Client sends an inquiry or project brief
    -> Thank-you pop-up + confirmation email to the client (copy of their answers)
    -> Alert email to you with every answer
    -> If their folder link is PRIVATE, the client is automatically asked to change it
       to "Anyone with the link can view", and you see a warning in the email and CRM
    -> Saved in your CRM with status "New"

  public/images/jeselle.jpg   your photo

STEP 2 — EMAIL SENDING (choose ONE)
  OPTION A — Brevo (free, no 2-Step Verification needed, 300 emails/day)
  1. Sign up free at brevo.com with bariljeselle@gmail.com and confirm your email.
  2. Senders, domains & dedicated IPs > Senders > make sure bariljeselle@gmail.com is
     added and verified (Brevo sends you a confirmation code/link).
  3. Settings > SMTP & API > SMTP tab > "Generate a new SMTP key" > copy it.
     Also copy the "Login" shown there (looks like 123abc@smtp-brevo.com).
  OPTION B — Gmail App Password (needs 2-Step Verification)
     myaccount.google.com/apppasswords > create > copy the 16 letters.

STEP 3 — UPLOAD TO NETLIFY (while LOGGED IN to app.netlify.com)
  Open your site (jeselleva) > Deploys > drag this WHOLE folder into the drop area.
  (Netlify installs the backend automatically. If the deploy log shows an error about
   functions, use the GitHub option at the bottom of this guide.)

STEP 4 — ADD 3 SETTINGS IN NETLIFY
  Site configuration > Environment variables > Add a variable:
    GMAIL_USER          bariljeselle@gmail.com   (the address emails come from)
    ADMIN_PASSWORD      (choose a password for your CRM page)
    If you chose Brevo:
    BREVO_LOGIN         the SMTP Login from Brevo (e.g. 123abc@smtp-brevo.com)
    BREVO_SMTP_KEY      the SMTP key from Brevo
    If you chose Gmail:
    GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD  the 16-letter App Password
  Then: Deploys > Trigger deploy > Deploy site  (so the settings take effect)

STEP 5 — TURN ON FORMS
  Netlify > Forms > Enable form detection (if asked) > redeploy once.

STEP 6 — TEST
  1. Open https://jeselleva.netlify.app/admin.html, enter your ADMIN_PASSWORD,
     click "Send test email" — you should receive it in Gmail.
  2. Book a test call on your site with another email address of yours.
     You should get: the thank-you pop-up, a confirmation email (client side),
     a "New booking" email (your side), and the booking in your CRM.

GOOGLE MEET LINKS (automatic, one time setup ~10 min)
  1. console.cloud.google.com → create project "Jeselle Website"
  2. APIs & Services → Library → enable "Google Calendar API" AND "Google Meet REST API"
  3. OAuth consent screen → External → app name, your email → add scopes calendar.events and meetings.space.created
     → Test users: add bariljeselle@gmail.com → then click "PUBLISH APP" (so the link never expires)
  4. Credentials → Create credentials → OAuth client ID → Web application
     Authorized redirect URI: https://jeselleva.netlify.app/api/google/callback
  5. Netlify → Environment variables: GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID and GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET → redeploy
  6. admin.html → "Connect Google Calendar" → sign in → Allow (ignore "unverified app": Advanced → Go to)
  Without this setup, every booking/inquiry/brief still gets a free instant video link (meet.jit.si) automatically.
  After this: every booking creates a Google Calendar event with a Meet link, invites the client,
  and puts a "Join Google Meet" button in both emails, reminders and the CRM.
  Inquiries & briefs: get their own Google Meet room link automatically (in both emails + the CRM).
  To set a time: open the record in the CRM → pick a time → "Schedule call + Meet link".

REVIEWS & FEEDBACK (reviews.html)
  Send happy clients this link:  https://jeselleva.netlify.app/reviews.html#write
  - Clients rate 1–5 stars, rate the details, pick highlights, write a review and a
    PRIVATE "what to improve" note (only you see it).
  - Reviews go live right away (like Shopee/Lazada) when the client allows it; you get an email.
  - admin.html → "Reviews & feedback": Hide, Publish again, reply publicly, or Delete

    you on Messenger/email (shown as "via Messenger"). Private feedback is never shown.
  - Clients can add a photo or logo (small circle). Remove it anytime with "Remove photo".
  - Published reviews also show on your homepage ("Kind words from clients").

OUT-OF-SCOPE CHECK (automatic)
  Every inquiry, booking note and project brief is scanned for requests that are not
  in your services (e.g. mobile apps, bookkeeping, running ads, fake followers).
  You'll see "⚠️ Heads up" at the top of the alert email and on the record in admin.html,
  with what the client wrote and what to do (🟢 add-on, 🟡 learnable, 🔴 decline).
  To change what gets flagged: netlify/lib/scope.mjs (or just ask Claude).

INCOME RECORDS (admin.html → 💰 Income records)
  Record every payment you receive, in pesos (what actually landed in GCash/Maya/Remitly).
  - From a payment proof: open it → "💰 Record as income" (it fills in the details and marks it Paid).
  - Anything else: "➕ Add payment received".
  - See this month, this year, and how close you are to the ₱250,000 tax-free limit.
  - "⬇️ Export for BIR (CSV)" downloads the year's list with a total, for your filing or accountant.
